📋 1. Verification & Order Lifecycle Scenarios

Sr. No. Scenario Sub-Scenario TAT / Action
1 Pending For Verification Before 24 Hours Will verify from OVS Panel.
2 Pending For Verification After 24 Hours Will verify from OVS Panel.
3 Within SLA Before Dispatch Share PDD.
4 Within SLA After Dispatch Share PDD, courier details and current location. Send AWB SMS.
5 Specific Request Before Dispatch Share PDD.
6 Specific Request After Dispatch Share PDD, courier details and current location.
7 Address Change Before Print Stage Share the changed address.
8 Address Change After Print Stage Ask the customer to coordinate with the courier if nearby.
9 Outside SLA Before Dispatch Raise a complaint. Team will provide an update.
10 Outside SLA After Dispatch Raise a complaint. Team will provide an update.

Sr. No. Scenario Sub-Scenario TAT / Action
1 Out For Delivery NA 09:00 PM
2 UD (Undelivered) All UD Reasons 24 Hours for re-attempt.
3 OFD After UD NA 09:00 PM
4 RTO Payment Mode – COD NA
5 RTO Payment Mode – Online 24–48 Hours
6 PDWP COD NA
7 PDWP Prepaid 7 Days
8 PDWP RFV + COD 24–48 Hours for update.

❌ 3. Cancellations & Refunds

Sr. No. Scenario Sub-Scenario TAT / Action
1 Why Was My Order Cancelled? COD Ask the customer to place a new order. If the customer agrees, place a new order through the CC Panel.
2 Why Was My Order Cancelled? Prepaid 24–48 Hours for refund initiation.
3 I Want to Cancel My Order Payment Mode – COD Doorstep refusal.
4 I Want to Cancel My Order Payment Mode – Online 6 Days after the order is marked RTO/Cancelled.
5 Failed Transaction Within TAT 72 Hours for auto reversal.

4 Complete Item Missing RPI = N/A or Blank 24–48 Hours for update.
5 Complete Item Missing Post 7 Days Share denial.
6 Complete Item Missing Complaint Within 7 Days, RPI = A (Approved) Ask for undertaking form.
7 Complete Item Missing RPI = D (Deny) Share denial.
8 Complete Item Missing RPI = N/A or Blank 24–48 Hours for update.
9 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) Post 7 Days Share denial.
10 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) Complaint Within 7 Days, RPI = A (Approved) 24–48 Hours for refund initiation.
11 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) RPI = D (Deny) Share denial.
12 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) RPI = N/A or Blank 24–48 Hours for update.
13 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) Post 7 Days Share denial.
14 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) Complaint Within 7 Days, RPI = A (Approved) Ask for undertaking form.
15 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) RPI = D (Deny) Share denial.
16 One or More Items Missing (Shipped Together) RPI = N/A or Blank 24–48 Hours for update.

🔄 5. Specialized Returns Scenarios

Sr. No. Scenario Sub-Scenario / Issue TAT / Action
1 DTSS (Returnable) UD / RTO / Cancelled Ask for packing, invoice and product images. 24 Hours post-images.
2 DTSS (Returnable) Delivered (DTSS OFD Stage) Ask for images. 24 Hours post-images.
3 Fresh Complaint Raised RPU Not Initiated 24 Hours for update.
4 Fresh Complaint Raised RPU Initiated, Pickup Partner Assigned 48 Hours for pickup. Share partner name.
5 Fresh Complaint Raised RPU Manual Assigned 48 Hours for update.
6 Fresh Complaint Raised RPU 48 Hours Breached, Product Not Picked 24 Hours for update.
7 Out for Pickup First / 2nd / 3rd / 4th OFP Wait until 6:00 PM. Share pickup partner name/number on call.
8 Pick Up Not Required NA NA
9 Pick Up Rescheduled NA 2–3 Days for pickup.
10 APUC Other State Customer 72 Hours for update.
11 APUC Rajasthan Customer Coordinate with pickup boy.
12 Multiple Suborder NA 24–48 Hours
13 Replacement Replacement Request 3–5 Days for replacement.
Important: Replacement TAT is 3–5 days. Share the correct timeline with the customer.

🧾 6. Post-Cancellation / Return / Refund Workflows

Sr. No. Scenario Sub-Scenario / Issue TAT / Action
1 CIP (Post-Cancellation) – 24–48 Hours for update.
2 CIP (Post-Return) – 24–48 Hours for update.
3 Refund Refund Initiated but Not Reflected (CIP Not Marked) Share the remaining TAT.
4 Refund Refund Not Initiated (XYZ Issue) 24–48 Hours for update.
5 Refund Refund Initiated, TAT Breached, Customer Complaint Ask for bank statement in PDF format.
6 Refund Went on Hold Log a Call 5 Days
7 Refund Went on Hold Refund "Unhold" Remarks Ask for UPI/bank details as per refund mode.

💰 7. Refund Mode Timelines

Sr. No. Refund Mode TAT
1 UPI to UPI 3–4 Days
2 Credit/Debit Card to Credit/Debit Card 7–10 Working Days
3 COD to Snapdeal Credit (RFV) Maximum 24 Hours
4 Snapdeal Credit (RFV) to Bank 8 Working Days
5 COD to Bank Refund (Online) Initiation: 24 Hours, Reflection: 2 Hours
6 COD to Bank Refund (Offline) 6 Days
7 COD to UPI (Online) Initiation: 24 Hours, instant reflection.
8 COD to UPI (Offline) 6 Days
