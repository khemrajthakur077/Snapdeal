🚚 1. Delivery & Pickup TAT

Sr. No. Scenario TAT / Customer Guidance
1 Product Out for Delivery (NDR) Customer ko raat 9:00 PM tak wait karne ke liye guide karein.
2 Product Out for Pickup (NPR) Customer ko shaam 6:00 PM tak wait karne ke liye guide karein.
3 CX Not Received Product but Marked as Delivered Within 7 Days: Customer ko inform karein ki team issue investigate karegi aur 7 days tak update provide karegi.

After 7 Days: Politely deny the request.
4 Undelivered Order – Reschedule Rescheduled delivery ke liye 24 Hours ka TAT.
5 Pickup Reschedule Same Return Reason: 2–3 Days

Fresh Pickup: 24 Hours
6 Replacement TAT Not Specified

💰 2. Refund TAT

Sr. No. Refund Mode / Scenario TAT / Customer Guidance
1 UPI 3–4 Days
2 Card Payments 7–10 Days
3 NEFT - Bank 5–7 Days
4 RFV - Refund Voucher / Snapdeal Wallet / Credit Instant Refund / once pickup is successful.
5 Customer Cancelled Product (Prepaid Order) Refund update 24–48 hours ke andar SMS ya WhatsApp ke through receive hoga.

24–48 Hours

📦 3. APUC – Already Picked Up but Status Showing Failed

Sr. No. Customer Location Action / TAT
1 Rajasthan Customer ko courier partner se apne end par contact karne ke liye guide karein.
2 Rajasthan ke alawa Other States Customer ko status update ke liye 72 Hours ki timeline dein.
Important: APUC ka matlab Already Picked Up hai, lekin system mein pickup status Failed show ho raha hai.
