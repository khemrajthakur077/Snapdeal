NDR – Non-Delivery Report

NDR campaign mein un customers ki calls aati hain jinke products undelivered hote hain. Is campaign mein customer ki concern handle karke order ko reattempt ke liye reschedule karna hota hai.

1. Campaign Overview

OVERVIEW

NDR ka full form Non-Delivery Report hai.

Is campaign mein humein un customers ki calls receive hoti hain jinke products undelivered hote hain. Customer ki concern handle karke order ko reattempt ke liye reschedule karna hota hai.

2. TAT – Turnaround Time

IMPORTANT

Normal Reattempt TAT

Normal reattempt ka TAT 24 Hours hai.

Product Out for Delivery

Agar product Out for Delivery hai, toh delivery attempt 9 PM tak expected hai.

7. Scenario – Delivered But Not Received

SPECIAL CASE

Ye scenario tab apply hota hai jab customer (CX) kehta hai ki usne product receive nahi kiya, lekin order status Delivered show ho raha hota hai.

Hamari responsibility customer ki concern check karna, timeline verify karna aur correct Kapture folders select karna hai.

CASE 1: CX Contacts Within 7 Days

Agar CX order Delivered mark hone ke 7 days ke andar contact karta hai, toh customer ko up to 7 days wait karne ke liye inform karein.

"We apologize for the inconvenience. We request you to kindly wait for up to 7 days while our team investigates the issue. We will provide you with an update within this timeline."

CASE 2: CX Contacts After 7 Days

Agar CX 7 days ke baad contact karta hai, toh applicable policy ke according politely request deny karni hai.

Important: 7-day investigation timeline sirf tab apply hoti hai jab CX 7 days ke andar contact karta hai.
