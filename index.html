Understanding E-commerce & Snapdeal

Welcome to the Snapdeal SOP Knowledge Hub. This dashboard introduces you to the world of e-commerce, how online marketplaces work, how Snapdeal connects customers and sellers, and how different teams help complete the customer's shopping journey.

Explore E-commerce ↓

What is E-commerce?

E-commerce means Electronic Commerce. It is the process of buying and selling products or services through the internet using websites, mobile applications and other digital platforms.

🛒

Online Shopping

Customers can search for products, compare prices, place orders and make payments using a website or mobile application.

🏪

Online Marketplace

A marketplace connects multiple sellers with customers through a common online platform. Sellers list their products and customers purchase them.

💳

Digital Payments

Customers may pay using supported payment methods such as UPI, cards, net banking or Cash on Delivery, depending on availability.

🚚

Delivery & Logistics

After an order is placed and processed, logistics partners help transport the product from the seller to the customer.

Types of E-commerce

E-commerce can be divided into different business models depending on who is buying and who is selling.

🏢

B2B – Business to Business

When one business sells products or services to another business.

Example: A wholesaler selling products to a retailer.

🛍️

B2C – Business to Consumer

When a business sells products directly to individual customers.

Example: A customer purchasing a product from an online shopping platform.

🤝

C2C – Consumer to Consumer

When one individual sells a product or service to another individual.

Example: Selling a used mobile phone to another person.

👤

C2B – Consumer to Business

When an individual provides a product or service to a business.

Example: A photographer licensing photographs to a company.

About Snapdeal

What is Snapdeal?

Snapdeal is an Indian e-commerce marketplace that connects online shoppers with sellers. It focuses on value commerce, offering budget-friendly fashion, lifestyle and other products to customers across India.

Snapdeal was founded in 2010 by Kunal Bahl and Rohit Bansal. The company initially started as a daily deals platform and later moved towards an online marketplace model.

Its focus is to make online shopping accessible for value-conscious customers, including shoppers in smaller cities and towns.

How Snapdeal Marketplace Works

Snapdeal works as an online marketplace. Sellers list their products on the platform, customers place orders, and the platform facilitates the buying and selling process.

1

Seller Registration

Sellers register and provide the required business and store details.

2

Product Listing

Sellers add product information, images, prices and other details.

3

Customer Order

Customers browse products and place orders on the platform.

4

Order Processing

The order goes through the required processing and verification stages.

5

Shipping

The product is handed over for delivery through the logistics process.

6

Delivery

The logistics partner attempts delivery to the customer's address.

Role of Customer Support in E-commerce

Customer support helps customers resolve issues related to their orders, products, delivery, returns and refunds.

OVS – Order Verification System

Helps verify customer orders and handle the relevant order verification process.

Address Correction

Handles customer requests related to incomplete or incorrect delivery addresses.

NDR – Non-Delivery Report

Handles cases where a customer's order could not be delivered.

NPR – Non-Pickup Report

Handles cases where a scheduled return pickup was not completed.

Learning Tip: Before handling any customer query, understand the customer's concern, check the available order details, follow the correct campaign SOP and select the appropriate tagging and disposition.
