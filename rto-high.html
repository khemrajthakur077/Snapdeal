Seller RTO High Campaign

Seller RTO High Campaign ke under woh sabhi calls aati hain jo un sellers ke orders se related hoti hain jinka RTO (Return to Origin) rate high hota hai. Is process mein system ki taraf se agent ko customer ki call connect hoti hai, jisme agent customer se order confirm karta hai aur ensure karta hai ki customer ne order place kiya hai aur woh order receive karna chahta hai. Iska main objective RTO rate ko reduce karna aur successful order delivery ensure karna hai.
