OVS Disposition Guide

This guide contains the available dispositions used across NPR, NDR, Inbound and OVS processes. It explains the purpose of each disposition along with its assigned priority, helping agents identify and select the appropriate disposition while updating customer interactions in Kapture.

Non-Pickup Related Dispositions

Disposition Definition Priority
Call Drop No voice, Disconnected during conversation 1
Listing issue If agent find listing issue in PDP 2
Bad Experience - Courier Cx stated bad experience - Courier boy rude, asked for money, asked to reach a location, fake comments 3
Bad Experience_Advisor Cx stated that he had bad experience with any of his previous communication with advisor or supervisor 4
Language barrier Call other than Tamil/Telugu and post Vernac window 5
Lang Barrier Call transfer If agent transfer the call to Vernac 6
